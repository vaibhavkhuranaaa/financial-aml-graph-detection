# Public replay fixture license

`data/fixtures/public_casefile.json` is the only committed data artifact in this
repository. It contains 55 selected and pseudonymized transactions across six
bounded cases. It is derived from IBM AML-Data v8 and is distributed under the
[Community Data License Agreement, Sharing, Version 1.0](https://cdla.dev/sharing-1-0/),
not the repository's MIT software license.

The artifact retains IBM and Erik Altman attribution, identifies its source,
records the approved distribution status, and validates its release identity
before the API serves it. Raw source files and the larger triage artifact are
not committed or published.
