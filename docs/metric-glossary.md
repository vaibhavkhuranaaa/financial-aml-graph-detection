# Metric glossary

One entry per number that appears anywhere in the product, the README, or a
portfolio page.

Every definition below was fixed before the number it describes existed. Where a
measurement has since been recorded as versioned evidence, the Result line
carries it and names the run record it came from.

Results are given for both variants the pipeline has been run on: HI-Small, the
evaluation variant, and LI-Small, the low prevalence sensitivity variant. Where a
Method line has drifted from what the code actually computes, it has been
corrected to the code and the correction is noted, because a definition that
describes a measurement nobody ran is worse than no definition.

## Template

**Metric** what it is called on screen.

- Definition: exact formula or rule.
- Method: data, splits, window, uncertainty.
- Direction: higher or lower is better.
- Baseline: what the simple alternative achieves.
- Result: the verified measurement.
- Decision: what changes because of it.
- Limitation: when it misleads.

## False positive reduction at held typology recall

- Definition: the share of the rules only alert volume that can be left unworked
  while the recall achieved by the rules engine is held, separately, for every
  typology rule. Reported as a percentage, as an absolute alert count, and as
  implied analyst hours.
- Method: time based split. Alerts generated by the rules engine over the
  training period, ranked, then evaluated on a later period. Confidence interval
  reported alongside the point estimate.
- Direction: higher is better.
- Baseline: **corrected on measurement.** The reference is the strongest rung of
  the ladder, which is B2 amount descending on HI-Small and B1 chronological on
  LI-Small, not B3. Decision records 0006 and 0014 are why.
- Result: **59.07 percent on HI-Small, 26.64 percent on LI-Small**, against a
  threshold of 20 percent. Coverage is held in true positive alerts rather than
  in attempts: at 25 surfaced attempts on HI-Small and 8 on LI-Small the attempt
  criterion is not estimable, and the attempt depths are published beside the
  alert depths rather than in place of them. Run records
  `data/backtest/challenger-1.json` and `data/li-small/backtest/challenger-1.json`.
- Decision: whether a ranker is promoted at all. If the interval crosses zero,
  the finding is no finding and no model ships.
- Limitation: it is a statement about a simulated alert population. It says
  nothing about real world detection, and it depends entirely on the rule
  parameters that produced the alerts.

## Precision at K

- Definition: of the top K ranked alerts, the count that resolve as suspicious.
  K is a stated analyst capacity in alerts per review period.
- Method: same split. Reported as a count first and a rate second.
- Direction: higher is better.
- Baseline: each rung of the ladder at the same K.
- Result: K is 126 alerts per period, 882 worked alerts over seven evaluation
  periods. On HI-Small, B0 6.92, B1 14.17, B2 15.31, B3 15.08 and C1 19.05
  percent. On LI-Small, B0 4.76, B1 16.10, B2 14.06, B3 14.17 and C1 17.23
  percent. Counts and 95 percent intervals are in the run records and in the
  README.
- Decision: sets the operating point shown by the capacity control in the
  product.
- Limitation: a rate quoted without its denominator is meaningless here.
  Precision of 10 percent on 200 worked alerts is 20 alerts.

## Per typology recall

- Definition: for each rule R1 through R8, the share of that rule's suspicious
  alerts captured within the worked depth.
- Method: computed and reported separately for every rule. Never reported only in
  aggregate.
- Direction: higher is better.
- Baseline: **corrected on measurement.** Recall is reported per injected
  typology against B3 at the same K, with the count of attempts the rules
  surfaced shown beside the count live in the period, because a rule targeting a
  pattern the simulator never generates has no recall to compare. The threshold
  is that no supported typology sits more than 5 points below B3.
- Result: **held on HI-Small at 0.0 points, failed on LI-Small at 10.00 points.**
  On HI-Small C1 is at or above B3 in every supported typology and recovers 12 of
  the 25 surfaced attempts against B3's 5. On LI-Small the rules surface 8
  attempts of 108 live, and B3 recovers one GATHER-SCATTER attempt of ten and one
  SCATTER-GATHER attempt of twelve where C1 recovers neither. Every per typology recall on that
  variant is zero or one attempt, so the criterion is decided by a single attempt
  landing above or below the cut line; it fails on the point values and that is
  what is reported.
- Decision: a ranker that raises the headline number while abandoning a typology
  is rejected regardless of the headline.
- Limitation: a rule targeting a pattern the simulator never generates shows a
  structural zero. That is a data property, not a model failure, and the two must
  be distinguished in every write up.

## Lift over baseline

- Definition: the ratio of a candidate's true positives found at K to the
  baseline's true positives found at the same K.
- Method: computed against every rung of the ladder, B0 random, B1 chronological,
  B2 amount descending, B3 rules only priority.
- Direction: higher is better.
- Baseline: 1.0, meaning no improvement. The ship gate is 1.3 against the
  strongest rung with the paired interval's lower bound above 1.0, and both
  halves have to hold.
- Result: **1.2444 on HI-Small, interval 1.1460 to 1.3359. 1.0704 on LI-Small,
  interval 0.9139 to 1.2441. Missed on both.** The strongest rung is B2 on
  HI-Small and B1 chronological on LI-Small, so the reference is variant specific
  under engine 2 and the LI-Small comparison is against arrival order. There the
  interval contains one, so C1 is not distinguishable from the rung at all. On
  HI-Small it clears the interval condition and the point estimate does not reach
  1.3. No model is promoted. Decision records 0007, 0008 and 0014.
- Decision: each rung must be beaten before the next candidate is justified.
- Limitation: lift over a weak rung is easy and means little. **Corrected on
  measurement:** the rung that carries the argument is B2, not B3, because B3 is
  itself beaten by sorting on a single feature. C1's lift over B3 is 1.451 on
  HI-Small and 1.4536 on LI-Small, both of which would clear the gate and neither
  of which is the comparison.

## Rank stability

- Definition: agreement between the ranked queue in consecutive review periods
  for alerts present in both.
- Method: **corrected on measurement.** What is computed is the Spearman
  correlation between the orderings two successive retrains assign to the same
  period, which is the question a retrained queue actually raises. The share of
  the top K that persists is not computed. Comparing consecutive periods would
  measure how much the alert population changed, not how much the model did.
- Direction: higher is better.
- Baseline: not applicable under the corrected method, which compares a model to
  itself. The threshold is a Spearman of 0.70.
- Result: **0.9317 on HI-Small and 0.9014 on LI-Small**, each a mean over six
  retrain pairs, against a threshold of 0.70. The queue would not reshuffle on
  retrain on either variant.
- Decision: an unstable queue is a product defect and blocks promotion even when
  the accuracy metrics look good.
- Limitation: high stability can also mean the ranker is ignoring new
  information. It is read alongside the accuracy metrics, never alone.

## Rules and model contribution split

- Definition: how much of the measured improvement comes from the rule set and
  how much from the ranker over it.
- Method: **corrected on measurement.** What is computed is the two ended split,
  rules only priority against the full model at the same K. The per feature
  family ablation was not run, because with ten periods and three of them the
  minimum training window there is no validation split that is not the evaluation
  set, so a family by family decomposition would be fitted to the periods it is
  reported on.
- Direction: informational, not optimised.
- Baseline: rules only.
- Result: **rules only accounts for 68.9 percent of C1's true positives on
  HI-Small and 68.79 percent on LI-Small.** The learned score adds 46 alerts and
  5.22 precision points on HI-Small, and 44 alerts and 4.99 points on LI-Small.
- Decision: answers whether a better tuned rule set would have delivered the same
  result without a model.
- Limitation: ablations are not additive when features are correlated, and the
  report says so rather than presenting a clean decomposition. The two ended
  split reported here attributes nothing to any individual feature and is not a
  decomposition at all.

## Calibration

- Definition: agreement between a stated probability and the observed frequency,
  read from a reliability curve.
- Method: computed only if a probability is surfaced to an analyst.
- Direction: closer to the diagonal is better.
- Baseline: the uncalibrated model output.
- Result: not measured, and not applicable while the product ranks only.
- Decision: whether a probability may be displayed at all.
- Limitation: a well calibrated probability on simulated data is calibrated to
  the simulation.
