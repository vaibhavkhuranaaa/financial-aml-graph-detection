# Signal Ledger 1.2.1

## Product

1.2.1 changes no number, no route, no contract and no artifact. Both pinned
digests are the ones 1.2.0 shipped. It fixes two things the browser journey found
when it was executed for the first time: a queue that took several hundred tab
stops to get out of, and an empty cell in the evidence funnel at narrow widths.

## Why

The triage queue renders every alert as its own control, 400 by default and 749
once the row budget is extended. Reaching the review record, the export or the
rule glossary below it meant tabbing through all of them. Nothing else on the
keyboard path was wrong: no positive tabindex anywhere, DOM order matching visual
order, a real ordered list, each row carrying its rank and values in its
accessible name, and the export staying out of the tab order while disabled. The
one missing thing was a way out.

A queue is the surface this project argues about most, so the exit had to say the
same thing the rest of the desk says. The link names the exact number of alerts it
passes and states that every one of them stays in the queue, because a control
that moves a reader past several hundred alerts should not be mistakable for one
that removes them. It is invisible until focused, since it answers a problem only
a keyboard reader has.

The funnel is three cells: attempts live, surfaced by the rules, reached at
capacity. It was laid out with `auto-fit`, which fits as many columns as the width
allows, so between roughly 413 and 602 pixels it fell into two columns and left
the fourth slot rendering as an empty bordered box. No number was wrong or
missing. The columns are now explicit: three across, and one down below 620
pixels, which removes the two column state that produced the gap rather than
patching its appearance.

## What changed

- `frontend/src/triage.tsx` gains the skip link above the queue and moves the
  landing target onto the region that actually follows it, which is the alert
  detail when one is open and the review record otherwise. Both carry an
  `aria-label`, so focus lands somewhere with a name to announce rather than on an
  empty div.
- `frontend/src/styles.css` styles the link in the established world, chartreuse
  on the dark ground with the same focus outline as every other control, and makes
  the funnel columns explicit.
- `scripts/verify_triage_browser.sh` gains four assertions: the link is hidden
  until focused, it names its count and says the alerts stay in the queue, it is
  visible once focused, and it lands on a named region.

## Verification

101 tests pass, ruff is clean, the frontend lints and builds, and both arms of
`scripts/verify_release.py` pass. The skip link and the funnel were both checked
in a browser against a locally served build of this release.

## Unchanged

The replay artifact and the triage artifact keep the digests pinned in
`src/app.py`. No route was added or removed. No measured number moved, and no
model ships: lift over the binding rung stays 1.2333 with an interval of 1.1045
to 1.3967 against a gate of 1.3.
