# Signal Ledger 1.2.0

## Product

1.2.0 adds the evidence the desk was reasoning without. The triage artifact now
carries the measured run broken back into the units the work happens in, and a
fourth typed GET route, `/api/triage/evidence`, serves it.

The queue, the capacity control, the cut line and the ladder are unchanged. What
changes is that a reader can now see where the alerts actually go.

## Why

The desk answered its question well and answered it for one review period in
pooled totals. Pooled figures hid three things that change what an investigation
lead would do about the result.

**The loss is upstream of the ranking.** 337 laundering attempts are live in the
evaluation window. The rules surface 25. The learned ranker reaches 8 of those
inside capacity, and rules only priority reaches 1. So 92.6 percent of the loss
happens before any ordering exists, and the ranking layer is competing for 25
attempts out of 337. That bound is a property of the alert population, and it is
the number that should govern the roadmap.

**Precision is carried by alerts no typology claims.** 198 of the 223 flagged
alerts in the population carry no typology attribution at all, and 139 of the
challenger's 148 true positives sit on that line. Its advantage over the binding
rung is 25 extra unattributed alerts and 3 extra attributed ones. The headline
metric is real and it is overwhelmingly not a statement about catching named
laundering structures.

**The periods are not alike and capacity is fixed.** Alert volume swings from 304
to 2,127 across the seven evaluation periods, a factor of 7. At a constant K of
126 that is coverage of 5.9 percent in the worst week against 41.4 percent in the
best. A pooled precision figure averages those together and hides the week that
actually breaks a team.

## What the evidence block carries

- **The funnel**: attempts live, attempts surfaced, attempts reached per rung, and
  the count lost before any ordering exists.
- **Per typology, both denominators**: recall against all attempts scores the
  rules; recall of surfaced attempts scores the ordering. They are different
  questions, and reporting only the first blames the ranker for a population it
  never had. FAN-OUT reads 4.4 percent against all attempts and 66.7 percent
  against the surfaced ones. Both are true.
- **The unattributed line** as its own block, because folding it into the typology
  table would put it in a denominator it does not belong to.
- **Per period**: alerts, coverage at K, true positives and precision per rung.
- **The volume claim, unpooled and signed.** The pooled alert based reduction is
  46.26 percent. Per period the attempt based measure is negative in 2 of 7
  periods, at minus 106 and minus 92, and one period had no attempt target at all.
  Quoting the pooled figure without the sign per period is the most misleading
  thing this project could do with its own data.
- **Rank stability**: Spearman correlation between consecutive orderings, 6 pairs,
  0.9034 to 0.9299 against a threshold of 0.70.
- **Feature importance**: the 39 model features ranked by gain, truncated to 15
  with the full count beside it. These are model feature names, not rule
  parameters, and no tuned trigger value is carried.

The per rule prior hit rates that B3 is built from are deliberately excluded. They
sit closer to the trigger set than to a published result, and the disclosure rule
in `docs/scope.md` governs them.

## Colour, and a defect that was avoided rather than fixed

The evidence block is the first part of this surface to encode identity in a mark
rather than in text. That changes what the palette has to survive.

The existing surface was not carrying a defect. Acid chartreuse and archive orange
appear on it as accent and status colours, and every element using them also
carries the words: a structural zero row says "Structural zero", a deferred row
says "Not reached at this capacity". Colour was never the only channel, so nothing
needed repairing.

Used as two adjacent series marks, though, those same hues collapse under
deuteranopia to an OKLab delta E of 1.8, and a red-green colourblind analyst could
not tell the binding baseline from the challenger. So the charts do not use them
that way. The challenger takes the chartreuse stepped into the dark lightness
band, the binding baseline takes blue, and orange is reserved for the capacity
line as a labelled status colour rather than a series. That set passes the
lightness band, chroma floor, colour vision separation, normal vision floor and
contrast checks on the adjacent pairlist.

Rank still carries no colour anywhere. That rule is untouched, and the charts do
not introduce one.

## Measured research result

Unchanged. **No model ships.** At K = 126 across seven evaluation periods, the
learned ranker finds 148 true positives in 882 worked alerts, precision 0.1678,
lift over the binding rung 1.2333 with a 95 percent interval of 1.1045 to 1.3967
against a precommitted gate of 1.3. The evidence block makes the size of that miss
more legible, not less. The challenger leads the binding rung in 7 of 7 periods,
which is a consistency claim and not an effect size claim, and the gate is an
effect size gate.
