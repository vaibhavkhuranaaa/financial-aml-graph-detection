# Signal Ledger 1.1.0

## Product

Signal Ledger 1.1.0 adds the alert triage desk to the public surface. The
approved evidence replay is unchanged: 6 bounded synthetic banking cases
containing 55 selected and pseudonymized transactions. Alongside it, the service
now serves one approved review period of the triage queue, 749 alerts carried
whole, with the capacity control in analyst hours, the cut line drawn inside the
queue, the ordering switch across every baseline rung and the challenger, alert
detail down to the contributing transactions, and both the structural zero and
baseline wins states.

The queue can be narrowed by typology and by disposition status. A narrowed view
changes what is on screen and nothing else: positions stay the alert's position
in the whole period, the cut line is still drawn, every capacity number is still
computed on the whole period, and the view states the count it is not showing.
The browser held review record can be exported to a local file.

## What the approval covers

The owner recorded a distribution decision for the triage slice against the exact
verified HI-Small source checksum. It covers **one review period of the HI-Small
variant and nothing else**: not another period, not the LI-Small variant, not the
alert store, not the feature table, and not the tuned rule parameter values. The
scope is written into the approval block rather than inferred from what was
built.

## Public boundary

- Read-only GET routes only.
- No request-time inference or live feed.
- No server-side visitor storage. Dispositions and their rationales stay in the
  browser, and the export writes a local file rather than uploading anything.
- No compliance recommendation, alert suppression, or customer action. The
  disposition control carries no default and there is no recommended action.
- Raw source data, the alert store, the feature table, tuned rule parameters,
  model execution, the LI-Small variant, and every review period other than the
  approved one remain local.

Both artifacts are pinned by content digest in `src/app.py`. An artifact that
does not match its pin is refused with HTTP 503 **even when it carries an
approved distribution decision**, because approval is a statement about a source
and any rebuild reading that source inherits the flag. The pin is public mode
only; `APP_MODE=local-triage-workbench` drops it so an operator can run an
artifact they just built, and keeps every other admission check.

## Measured research result

No model ships, and 1.1.0 changes nothing about that. At K = 126 across seven
evaluation periods, the learned ranker finds 148 true positives in 882 worked
alerts, with precision 0.1678. Its lift over the strongest baseline, B2 amount
descending, is 1.2333 with a 95 percent interval of 1.1045 to 1.3967. That misses
the precommitted 1.3 ship gate. The negative result reproduces on LI-Small, where
lift is 1.2818 and the per-typology floor also fails. The surface states this
above the queue in the largest type on the page.
