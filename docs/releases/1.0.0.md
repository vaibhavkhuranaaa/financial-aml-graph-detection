# Signal Ledger 1.0.0

## Product

Signal Ledger 1.0.0 is the approved public evidence replay: 6 bounded synthetic
banking cases containing 55 selected and pseudonymized transactions. Reviewers
can inspect a deterministic sequence, its counterparties, evidence, and source
lineage, then save a simulated rationale inside their browser.

## Public boundary

- Read-only GET routes only.
- No request-time inference or live feed.
- No server-side visitor storage.
- No compliance recommendation, alert suppression, or customer action.
- Raw source data, the alert store, tuned rule parameters, model execution, and
  the larger triage artifact remain local.

The public service returns HTTP 503 for the triage route. The larger artifact is
not included in this release and its distribution status remains pending owner
approval.

## Measured research result

No model ships. At K = 126 across seven evaluation periods, the learned ranker
finds 148 true positives in 882 worked alerts, with precision 0.1678. Its lift
over the strongest baseline, B2 amount descending, is 1.2333 with a 95 percent
interval of 1.1045 to 1.3967. That misses the precommitted 1.3 ship gate. The
negative result reproduces on LI-Small, where lift is 1.2818 and the per-typology
floor also fails.
